Good questions

What should we wear?

Calm colours that go together, without big logos. After booking I send you a short guide with outfit ideas

What if it rains?

We move the shoot to another day for free, or find a cosy place indoors

My kids won't sit still. Is that a problem?

Not at all. They don't have to. I work with games and movement, and the most honest photos come from that

How do I book a date?

Send a message through the form or Instagram. A deposit holds the date, the rest you pay on the day of the shoot

Can I order a photobook?

Yes. I design a photobook with your photos and order the print, so you can keep it or gift it
